Why: macOS exposes no GPU fan control API. The SMU firmware follows the
VBIOS fan table; factory zero-RPM keeps the fans stopped below 55 C, so
macOS idles near 50 C. With zero-RPM disabled the fans run at FanPwmMin
(~25%) continuously and idle drops to roughly 40 C.
